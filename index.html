          Aim for hard buffer of at least 10% and total slack around 20–30%. Below that the plan is brittle; above 40% it isn't a plan. The startup and learning lengths in Settings are the dials.

          Notifications

          In-app reminders work while the planner is open: a tab (even in the background), or the installed app while your phone keeps it running. Phones pause web apps after a few minutes in the background or with the screen locked. For reminders that always fire, use calendar reminders below.

          Block types, colors and reminders

          The color is used everywhere: timeline, now-bar, task labels, notifications. Lead = minutes before the block starts.

          Calendar reminders (planner closed, phone locked)

          Exports your blocks (only types with reminders on) as a calendar file with alarms. Import it into your phone's calendar and the calendar app reminds you, offline, with no server. Re-export when the plan changes, for example in the Saturday weekly review.

          Account and sync

          Your phone and laptop share the same data once both are signed in. Edits made offline upload when you reconnect; if both devices changed the same thing, the later edit wins.

          Backup
